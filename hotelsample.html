Our Heritage

A Century of Refined Living

Born from a vision of timeless elegance, The Grandveil has welcomed heads of state, celebrated artists, and discerning travelers since 1924. Each corner of our hotel carries the weight of history and the warmth of genuine care.

Our 87 rooms and suites are curated sanctuaries — a seamless blend of Belle Époque architecture and contemporary comfort, where every detail has been considered with obsessive precision.
